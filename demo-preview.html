Frequently Asked Questions

System facts & deployment details.

What hardware does it run on?
A dedicated Mac Mini installed at your location or ours. It's not shared infrastructure — it's YOUR machine, running YOUR AI employee 24/7.
Is it really 24/7?
Yes. The hardware never turns off. Your AI employee monitors channels, responds to leads, and manages operations around the clock. If something needs your attention, it alerts you.
How is this different from a chatbot?
Chatbots follow scripts. Your AI employee thinks. It reads context, makes decisions, coordinates across platforms, and learns your business. It's closer to a remote employee than a chat widget.
Can I customize what it does?
Completely. We configure every workflow to your business — which channels it monitors, how it responds, what it escalates to you, how it books appointments. Nothing is one-size-fits-all.
What's the setup process?
We start with a free audit of your operations. Then we build your AI employee's logic, deploy the hardware, and go live. Most businesses are fully operational within 2 weeks.
What if I want to cancel?
Month-to-month. No contracts, no cancellation fees. We earn your business every month.
