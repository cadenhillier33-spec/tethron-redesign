    Frequently Asked Questions

    Straight answers to the questions we get every week.

    How is this different from a chatbot?
    Chatbots follow scripts. A Tethron AI employee reads context, makes judgment calls, coordinates across Gmail / CRM / calendar / content / ops, and learns your business over time. Chatbot = vending machine. AI employee = real team member.
    How fast until it's live?
    3 days. Day 1: environment setup, tool connections, voice training. Day 2: workflow build, logic, soft testing. Day 3: handoff, go live, first-week monitoring. No 6-week onboarding. No "kickoff call" theater.
    Is it really 24/7?
    Yes. Your AI employee never clocks out. Leads answered in 60 seconds at any hour, follow-ups run for months, content drafted while you sleep. Anything urgent gets escalated to you directly.
    Can I customize what it does?
    Completely. Every workflow is configured to your business — channels monitored, response tone, what it escalates, what it handles autonomously. Draft-and-flag mode if you want approval on every send. Autonomous mode once you trust it. Changeable anytime.
    What's the difference between Division and Professional?
    Division is a pre-trained operator from our roster of 14 proven roles — SDR, Exec Assistant, Bookkeeper, Legal Assistant, Receptionist, and more. You pick one, deploy in 3 days, flat pricing.

    Professional is a custom AI employee built entirely around your business — trained on your voice, your workflows, your stack. Cross-tool coordination, strategic recommendations, no templates.

    Pick Division if a proven out-of-the-box role covers it. Pick Pro if the role doesn't exist in the roster, or you want it built around YOU.
    Can I upgrade from Division to Pro later?
    Yes, whenever you're ready. Start with a pre-built operator from the roster — and when your business outgrows it (custom workflows, deeper training on your ops, or a role the roster doesn't cover) — we rebuild it as a Pro custom employee. Nothing lost. Just evolved.
    Do I need the Enterprise Mac Mini?
    Probably not. Enterprise is the only tier with physical on-site hardware — dedicated Mac Mini, real-time voice agent, direct iMessage from your number, quarterly strategy sessions. It's built for data-sensitive industries (legal, healthcare, finance), compliance-heavy operations, or owners who want full hardware sovereignty.

    Division and Pro both run in the cloud — that's more than enough for 90% of businesses.
    What if I want to cancel?
    Month-to-month. No contracts, no cancellation fees. We earn your business every month or you walk. Setup fee comes with a 24-hour refund window.
